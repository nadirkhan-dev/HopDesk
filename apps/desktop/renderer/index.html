Sign in to a HopDesk server

A HopDesk server is one you or your organisation runs. It lists your computers so you can connect to them from anywhere, and passes connections along when two computers cannot reach each other directly. It never sees what is on your screen.

Servers ask for this so that only people who were given it can create an account.
